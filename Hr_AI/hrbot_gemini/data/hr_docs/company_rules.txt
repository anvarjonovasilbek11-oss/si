Company Rules and Code of Conduct

1. Professional Conduct
All employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional conduct at all times. This includes:
- Treating colleagues, clients, and visitors with respect and courtesy
- Maintaining a professional appearance appropriate to your role
- Using professional language in all business communications
- Avoiding conflicts of interest

2. Working Hours
- Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday
- Employees must clock in and out using the attendance system
- Late arrivals (more than 15 minutes) must be reported to your supervisor
- Flexible working arrangements may be available with manager approval

3. Attendance and Punctuality
- Regular attendance and punctuality are essential
- Absences must be reported to your manager before 9:00 AM on the day of absence
- Excessive absenteeism may result in disciplinary action
- Three consecutive days of unexplained absence may be considered job abandonment

4. Workplace Safety
- Follow all safety protocols and procedures
- Report any hazards or safety concerns immediately
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE) where required
- Participate in mandatory safety training sessions

5. Confidentiality and Data Protection
- Protect company confidential information and trade secrets
- Do not share sensitive information with unauthorized parties
- Follow data protection policies when handling customer information
- Use secure methods for transmitting confidential data

6. Use of Company Property
- Company equipment and resources are for business use only
- Personal use of company property should be minimal and reasonable
- Return all company property upon termination of employment
- Report any damaged or lost company property immediately

7. Internet and Email Usage
- Internet and email should be used primarily for work-related purposes
- Do not access inappropriate or offensive content
- Do not send spam or chain emails
- Be aware that company systems may be monitored

8. Harassment and Discrimination
- The company has zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination
- All employees have the right to work in a harassment-free environment
- Report any incidents of harassment to HR immediately
- Violations will result in serious disciplinary action, including termination

9. Substance Abuse
- The workplace must be free from alcohol and illegal drugs
- Employees under the influence will be sent home and may face disciplinary action
- Smoking is only permitted in designated areas

10. Disciplinary Procedures
Progressive discipline may include:
- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Suspension
- Termination

Serious violations may result in immediate termination without prior warnings.

For clarification on any company rules, please contact the HR department.
