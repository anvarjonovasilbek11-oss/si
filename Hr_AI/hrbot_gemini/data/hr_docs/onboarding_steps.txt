Employee Onboarding Process

Welcome to the team! This document outlines the onboarding process for new employees.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST DAY

1. Documentation
- Complete and return all employment forms
- Provide copies of required identification documents
- Submit banking details for salary payments
- Complete tax declaration forms
- Sign the employment contract and confidentiality agreement

2. Background Checks
- Background verification will be conducted
- Reference checks with previous employers
- Educational qualification verification

YOUR FIRST DAY

3. Welcome and Orientation (9:00 AM - 10:00 AM)
- Meet with HR for welcome session
- Receive employee ID badge and access cards
- Complete emergency contact forms
- Review employee handbook

4. IT Setup (10:00 AM - 11:00 AM)
- Receive laptop and necessary equipment
- Set up email account and system access
- Install required software and applications
- IT security briefing and password setup

5. Workspace Setup (11:00 AM - 12:00 PM)
- Tour of office facilities
- Introduction to your workspace
- Overview of office amenities (cafeteria, parking, etc.)
- Emergency evacuation procedures

6. Lunch with Team (12:00 PM - 1:00 PM)
- Meet your team members
- Informal introduction and networking

7. Department Introduction (1:00 PM - 3:00 PM)
- Meet with your manager
- Overview of department structure and goals
- Introduction to key team members
- Review of initial responsibilities

8. HR Policies and Benefits (3:00 PM - 4:00 PM)
- Overview of company policies
- Benefits enrollment (health insurance, retirement plans)
- Leave policies and procedures
- Performance review process

9. First Day Wrap-up (4:00 PM - 5:00 PM)
- Q&A session
- Schedule for first week
- Assign onboarding buddy/mentor

FIRST WEEK

10. Training Schedule
- Day 2: Product/Service training
- Day 3: Systems and tools training
- Day 4: Compliance and safety training
- Day 5: Shadow experienced team member

11. Key Meetings
- One-on-one with direct manager
- Team meeting introduction
- Cross-functional team introductions

FIRST MONTH

12. 30-Day Check-in
- Meeting with HR to discuss progress
- Feedback session with manager
- Address any questions or concerns
- Review of probationary period expectations

13. Ongoing Development
- Identify training needs
- Set initial performance goals
- Establish regular one-on-one schedule with manager

PROBATIONARY PERIOD

14. 90-Day Review
- Formal performance evaluation
- Discussion of strengths and areas for improvement
- Decision on permanent employment status
- Set goals for the next quarter
