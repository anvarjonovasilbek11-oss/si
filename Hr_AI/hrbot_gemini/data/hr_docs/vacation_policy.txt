Vacation and Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave Entitlement
All full-time employees are entitled to 15 days of paid annual leave per year. Part-time employees receive prorated leave based on their working hours.

2. Leave Accrual
Leave days accrue monthly at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Employees can start using accrued leave after completing their probationary period (90 days).

3. Leave Request Process
- All leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal
- Requests are subject to manager approval
- Emergency leave requests will be considered on a case-by-case basis
- Employees should receive approval confirmation within 3 business days

4. Carryover Policy
Unused leave days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any leave beyond this limit will be forfeited at the end of the calendar year.

5. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

6. Public Holidays
The company observes all national public holidays. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, a compensatory day off will be provided.

7. Unpaid Leave
Employees may request unpaid leave for personal reasons. Such requests must be approved by both the direct manager and HR department.

8. Leave During Notice Period
Employees serving their notice period are generally not permitted to take leave unless approved by management.

For questions about leave policies, please contact the HR department at hr@company.com or extension 1234.
